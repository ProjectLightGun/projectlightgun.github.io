Project Light Gun: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Project Light Gun ("the app") is made by GameOnScorpio, a one-person developer based in the UK ("I", "me"). The short version: the app doesn't collect anything about you.

What I collect

Nothing from the app. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The only time I get any personal information is if you email me.

Buying the app

You buy it through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta handles your account and payment under its own privacy policy. I don't receive your name, email or payment details.

What stays on your headset

  • Your games, BIOS files, saves and settings stay on your Quest. They are never sent to me or anyone else.
  • Hand Occlusion uses the headset's cameras and hand tracking to show your hands in front of the screen. This happens live on the headset; nothing is saved or sent.
  • File access: the app only reads its own ProjectQLG folder, and your Download folder when you ask it to import games.

When the app uses the internet

  • Emulators: the first time you open a system, the app downloads its emulator from a public GitHub page. GitHub sees the request (including your IP address), as with any download.
  • Cover art: pictures come from the libretro thumbnail library. The request includes the game's name and your IP address.

Features on your own Wi-Fi

Transfer over Wi-Fi and Watch on Another Screen let another device on your Wi-Fi connect to the headset. They're off until you turn them on, and nothing goes over the internet. Anyone on the same Wi-Fi who knows the address can watch while Watch on Another Screen is on.

If you email me

I use your email only to reply, and delete it within 12 months of our last message. My email is Gmail, so Google may store it outside the UK.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what I hold about you, or ask me to correct or delete it, by emailing me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The app is for ages 13 and over. If a child has emailed me and a parent wants it deleted, just contact me.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
