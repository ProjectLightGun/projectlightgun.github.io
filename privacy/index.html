Project Light Gun: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Project Light Gun ("the app") is made by GameOnScorpio ("I", "me"). This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: I don't collect any of it.

What I collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. I don't receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your headset

  • Your game files, BIOS files, save files and settings are stored only on your Quest, in the app's folders. They are never sent to me or anyone else.
  • Camera and hand tracking. If you turn on Hand Occlusion, the app reads the headset's camera and hand-tracking data so your hands and controller can appear in front of the game screen. This is processed live on the headset. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • File access. The app asks for access to files on the headset so it can find the games you add. It only reads the app's own folder and your Download folder, when you ask it to import.

When the app uses the internet

  • Downloading emulator cores. The first time you open a system, the app downloads that system's emulator from a public GitHub page. GitHub can see the request, such as your IP address, as for any download. I don't see it.
  • Game cover art. The game list may download cover pictures from a public image library. That library can see which pictures were requested.

Features that stay on your own network

  • Transfer over Wi-Fi and Watch on Another Screen run a small web page on your headset so another device on the same Wi-Fi can connect. They are off until you turn them on, and they work only on your local network. Nothing goes through me or the internet.

Children

The app is for ages 13 and over. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
