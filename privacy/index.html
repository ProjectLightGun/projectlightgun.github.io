Project Light Gun: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how Project Light Gun ("the app") handles information. The app is developed by GameOnScorpio, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us", "our").

In summary: the app does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information. It has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us by email.

We never sell, rent or share your personal information.

Purchases

The app is sold through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta processes your account and payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Crash reports

If your headset is set to share crash reports with Meta, Meta may make technical crash reports for the app available to us through its developer dashboard. These reports describe the app's behaviour and are used only to fix problems.

Information stored on your headset

  • Games and settings: your game files, BIOS files, saves and settings are stored only on your headset. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Hand Occlusion: when this feature is turned on, the app uses the headset's cameras and hand tracking to show your hands in front of the game screen. This is processed in real time on the headset. No images are saved or transmitted.
  • File access: the app reads only its own ProjectQLG folder, and your Download folder when you choose to import games.

Internet connections

  • Emulators: the first time you open a system, the app downloads the required emulator from a public GitHub repository. GitHub receives the request, including your IP address, as with any download.
  • Cover art: game cover images are downloaded from the libretro thumbnail library. The request includes the game's name and your IP address.

Local network features

Transfer over Wi-Fi and Watch on Another Screen allow another device on the same Wi-Fi network to connect to your headset. Both are off until you turn them on, and no data passes over the internet. While Watch on Another Screen is on, anyone on the same network who knows the address shown in the app can view the stream.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you, as we have a legitimate interest in supporting users of the app. We delete this correspondence within 12 months of our last exchange. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which may store it outside the United Kingdom.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. To make a request, email us; we will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. If a parent or guardian believes their child has contacted us, they can ask us to delete that correspondence.

This website

This page does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
